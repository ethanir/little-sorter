Support

Little Sorter

Need help? Email us anytime at littlesorterapp@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Which worlds are free?

Farm and Ocean are always free. Town, Jungle, Vehicles, and Garden unlock together with a single one-time purchase — no subscriptions.

How do I unlock all the worlds?

Tap the small Grown-Ups button at the bottom of the main screen, answer the parent question, then choose Unlock all worlds.

I already bought it, but the worlds are locked (or I got a new device)

Go to Grown-Ups and tap Restore purchase. Your unlock is tied to your Apple Account, so it restores for free.

What is Challenge Mode?

It's an optional mode that adds a gentle 30-second timer and one-to-three star rewards for older children. It's off by default and can be turned on under Grown-Ups.

Does the app have ads or collect data?

No. Little Sorter has no ads, no tracking, and collects no data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Does it need the internet?

No — the whole game works offline. A connection is only used briefly if you choose to make or restore a purchase.
