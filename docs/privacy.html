Privacy Policy

Little Sorter · Effective May 26, 2026

The short version: Little Sorter collects nothing. No personal data, no tracking, no ads, no accounts, and no internet connection is used to play. Everything stays on your device.

Little Sorter is a game made for young children, and we built it so you never have to worry about privacy. This policy explains exactly how the app handles information — which is to say, it doesn't gather any.

Information we collect

None. Little Sorter does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from anyone, including children. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party software development kits of any kind.

Where your progress is stored

Your child's progress — completed worlds, best times, and stars — is saved only on the device itself, using the device's standard local storage. It never leaves the device and is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes it.

Children's privacy

Little Sorter is designed for children and is committed to protecting their privacy. Because the app collects no personal information whatsoever, it is built to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Apple's Kids Category requirements.

Purchases

Little Sorter offers a single optional one-time purchase to unlock additional worlds. All purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase information is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Third parties

There are none. Little Sorter contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services, and shares no information with third parties.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
